Trust & Safety

BorrowX is designed around safer community exchanges.

Important safety rules
  • Use only an approximate area in public listings.
  • Never publish your exact home address.
  • Never publish your personal phone number.
  • Prefer safe, public locations for handovers.
  • Do not meet unknown people alone if you feel unsafe.
  • Use BorrowX chat instead of posting personal contact details.
  • Report suspicious listings or accounts.
  • Verification and ratings do not guarantee that another person is completely safe.
BorrowX is a community platform. Use your judgment and involve a trusted adult when appropriate.
